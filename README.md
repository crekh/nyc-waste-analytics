# nyc-waste-analytics

**Project Overview**
This project investigates NYC's waste management efficiency over a decade. By merging DSNY operational data with demographic census data, I identified critical disparities in how different boroughs participate in diversion (recycling/composting) programs.

**Analytical Focus**
* **The Problem**: Despite city-wide mandates, recycling participation is not uniform across NYC’s five boroughs.

* **The Data**: 10 years of time-series waste records (2013–2023) combined with socio-economic census indicators.

* **The Solution**: An end-to-end Tableau storyboard that guides stakeholders through the "Who, Where, and Why" of NYC waste patterns.

**Key Insights Uncovered**
* **Seasonal Volatility**: Identified specific months where waste generation peaks, putting a strain on collection infrastructure.

* **Service Deserts**: Mapped accessibility gaps where residents have the lowest proximity to diversion facilities.

* **Borough Disparities**: Quantified the correlation between census demographics and diversion rates to highlight areas requiring better sustainability outreach.
